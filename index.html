Getting Started

This website creates a detailed search prompt for your favorite AI assistant.

  1. You will need an active AI account, such as ChatGPT or Gemini.
  2. In the left panel, choose what you want to watch and adjust the search options. Some choices are already selected; click More to see additional streaming services.
  3. Review Your Search Summary in the right panel, which updates automatically.
  4. Click Step 2 to copy the complete prompt, including detailed instructions added automatically.
  5. Open ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, or another AI assistant. Start a new chat, paste the prompt, and send it.

Results are designed for U.S. streaming services. Because availability changes, verify titles before renting or purchasing.
